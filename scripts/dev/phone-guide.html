Catalog on an iPhone

Setting up Dad's iPhone with Mom's laptop, so he can watch anywhere, and keep replays on his phone to watch at work with no Wi-Fi or signal.

Mom's laptopCatalog runs here (the TVs use it too)
↔
Tailscalea private link between the family's devices, at home or away
↔
Dad's iPhoneCatalog on the Home Screen, with his own profile

Everything here is free. Part 1 is done once on Mom's laptop; Part 2 once on each phone. It takes about 15 minutes.

Before starting: Mom's laptop needs the Catalog version that has Settings → Phones. If that section isn't there, update Catalog first (Settings → Updates).

Part 1 · Mom's laptop · onceTailscale and the phone address

1. Install Tailscale

  1. On the laptop, go to tailscale.com/download and download Tailscale for Windows. Install it.
  2. Click Log in and sign in. Use one account for the whole family (for example Mom's Google account). Every phone will sign in with this same account.
  3. Tailscale's icon appears by the clock. It should say Connected.

2. Turn on the phone address in Catalog

  1. Open Catalog. Go to Settings → Phones.
  2. Click Turn on the phone address.
  3. The first time only, Catalog may say Tailscale wants HTTPS turned on. Click the link it shows, sign in with the family Tailscale account, click Enable, then go back to Catalog and click Turn on the phone address again.
  4. Catalog now shows a square code and an address like https://moms-laptop.tail1234.ts.net. Leave this page open for Part 2.

3. Keep the laptop awake

  1. Windows Settings → System → Power: when plugged in, put the device to sleep after Never.
  2. Keep it plugged in, with Catalog open (minimized is fine). If the laptop sleeps or Catalog is closed, phones and TVs can't reach it, but downloads kept on a phone still play.

Part 2 · Dad's iPhone · onceTailscale, Catalog on the Home Screen, his profile

1. Tailscale on the iPhone

  1. Open the App Store, search Tailscale, and install it (free).
  2. Open it, tap Log in, and sign in with the same family account as the laptop.
  3. When the iPhone asks to Add VPN Configurations, tap Allow (and enter the passcode).
  4. In Tailscale's settings, turn on VPN On Demand so it reconnects by itself. The laptop should be listed in the app as connected.

2. Open Catalog and add it to the Home Screen

  1. Open the iPhone's Camera and point it at the square code on the laptop's Settings → Phones page. Tap the yellow link that appears. (Or type the address into Safari.)
  2. Catalog opens in Safari (if it asks Who's watching?, tap Dad). Tap the Share button (the square with the arrow), then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Close Safari. From now on, always open Catalog from its Home Screen icon: only that one keeps downloads on the phone.

3. Who's watching

  1. Open Catalog from the Home Screen. It asks Who's watching? once more (the Home Screen app keeps its own memory, apart from Safari).
  2. Tap Dad. If he doesn't have a profile yet, tap Add profile, type his name, and tap Add.
  3. That's remembered on his phone. It's the same profile as on the TVs, so what he watches on the phone shows in his Continue Watching on the TV, and the other way round.
  4. To change it later, tap the picture with his initial at the top of the library.

Every time · at homeKeeping a replay on the phone

  1. At home (on Wi-Fi), open Catalog from the Home Screen and find the game, film or episode.
  2. Start it playing, then tap Download at the top and pick the quality (and captions if wanted). The laptop downloads it; this can take a few minutes. Watch it in Downloads.
  3. When it shows its size (no more percent), tap Keep on this phone.
  4. Keep Catalog open and the screen on until it says On this phone. (An iPhone pauses a web app in the background; if it stops, tap Keep on this phone again and it carries on from where it got to.)

How much room? A 3-hour game in 720p is about 2–4 GB. The iPhone's Settings → General → iPhone Storage shows what's free.

At work · no connectionWatching

  1. Open Catalog from the Home Screen. With no connection it opens a simple list: what's kept on this phone.
  2. Tap one to play it, with its captions. The phone remembers where he stopped.
  3. Back at home, opening Catalog sends those places to the laptop, so Continue Watching is right on the TVs too.
  4. When it's watched, Downloads → Remove from phone frees the room (it stays on the laptop).
